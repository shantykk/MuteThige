About the Firm

A Firm Built on Integrity, Practicality & Results

Susan Mute & Co Advocates is a client-centred law firm based in Kiambu, Kenya. We provide practical legal support in compliance, property, commercial and civil matters, family law, data privacy, and policy drafting — with clear guidance, timely responses, and integrity at every step.

At Susan Mute & Co Advocates, we believe good legal counsel starts with genuinely understanding our clients — their goals, their risks, and what's really at stake. That philosophy shapes everything we do.

Led by Managing Partner Susan Mute, Advocate of the High Court of Kenya, our team is committed to accessible, timely, and professional service — because whether you're an individual navigating a personal matter or a corporate client managing complex risk, you deserve counsel that's as thorough as it is responsive.

“Our mission is simple: to make excellent legal representation accessible, timely, and rooted in genuine care for every client we serve.” — Susan Mute, Managing Partner
